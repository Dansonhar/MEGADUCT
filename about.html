Our Approach

How a MEGADUCT system comes together

Every run we ship follows the same path — from the consultant's specification to a type-tested system installed on site.

Step 01

Specification & Design

We review load schedules, riser geometry and fault levels, then configure the system — feeder runs, plug-in sections, elbows and transposition where the route demands it.

Step 02

Thermodynamic Engineering

Sophisticated thermodynamic design applications govern conductor sizing and enclosure design so temperature rise stays within limits under continuous load.

Step 03

Quality Manufacturing

Components are fabricated in Beranang, Selangor under quality manufacturing methods, with accessories and special items built to the project's exact geometry.

Step 04

Delivery & Site Support

Systems ship to site with joint sets, hangers and clips as a coordinated package — our key personnel stay involved through installation and commissioning.
