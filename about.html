About Us

COMPANY BACKGROUND

MEGADUCT busduct trunking systems have been established since 1992 and have successfully supplied many projects to overseas and local market.

With the continued product research and development process, we are able to provide uniquely and highly reliable products. This is achieved by adopting sophisticated thermodynamic design applications and quality manufacturing methods to deliver high performance products to our customers.

Our track record shows the dedication and determination of our key personnel as they turn their dreams into reality.

HOW WE WORK

  • We review your requirement

    Load schedules, riser geometry and fault levels, so the system is configured correctly before anything is made.

  • We engineer the system

    Thermodynamic design applications govern conductor sizing and enclosure design, so temperature rise stays within limits under continuous load.

  • We manufacture it

    Built at our factory in Beranang, Selangor. Special items are made to the exact geometry your building requires.

  • We support you on site

    The system is delivered as one coordinated package. Our people stay involved through installation and commissioning.
