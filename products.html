THE THREE GROUPS

Standard Items

Flange end, feeder and plug-in runs, flatwise, edgewise, offset and tee elbows, and the end feed cable box. These form the main route from the switchgear to where the power is needed.

Busduct Accessories

Joint sets, vertical hangers, horizontal clips and tap-off units. These join the run together, hold it to the building, and let you take power off it.

Special Items

Flange-and-elbow combinations, offset elbows made to a given angle, and transposition units. We make these when the standard shapes will not fit your building.

Discuss your project with us

Send us your drawings and load schedule. Our engineers will advise on the right busduct configuration for the job.
