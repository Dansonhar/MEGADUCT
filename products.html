Standard Items

Seven core components — flange ends, feeder and plug-in runs, flatwise, edgewise, offset and tee elbows, and the end feed cable box — forming the backbone of any distribution route.

Busduct Accessories

Joint sets, vertical hangers, horizontal clips and tap-off units — the fixings and take-off points that turn sections into an installed, serviceable system.

Special Items

Flange-and-elbow combinations, offset elbows to a specified degree and transposition units — fabricated when standard geometry will not fit the building.
