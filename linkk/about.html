01 Company story

From one factory in Beranang.

We are a leading bus duct trunking systems manufacturer. Two dates matter: the year the busduct went into production, and the year the present company was incorporated around it.

1992Origin

Manufacturing begins

Our journey commenced in 1992. MEGADUCT busduct trunking systems have been established since then and have successfully supplied many projects to overseas and local markets.

2013Incorporated

LINKK Busway Systems (M) Sdn Bhd

The present company — Company No. 1018052-D — was established in 2013 and holds the exclusive trademark for MEGADUCT, the busduct brand manufactured at our factory in Beranang, Selangor.

02 Where we work

Two regions, one factory.

Everything is designed and built in Beranang and shipped from there. Our regional presence across Asia Pacific and the Middle East runs through an established distributor network, which is how a system specified in one country gets supported in another.
