About Us

Who we are

We are a leading bus duct trunking systems manufacturer.

Our journey commenced in 1992. Since then we have built a track record of more than 5,000 completed projects across over 50 countries, and we maintain a strong regional presence throughout Asia Pacific and the Middle East through an established distributor network.

LINKK Busway Systems (M) Sdn Bhd was established in 2013 and holds the exclusive trademark for MEGADUCT, the busduct brand manufactured at our factory in Beranang, Selangor.

Our vision

To become a global leader in bus duct manufacturing, consistently exceeding customer expectations through superior systems and service.

Our mission

  • Continuously improve product quality and reliability
  • Expand our product and service offering
  • Broaden our global market reach
  • Invest in advanced research and development
  • Support the growth and development of our team

Our people

Our people are our strength. What the company achieves depends on the talent and dedication of its team, and we support their development accordingly.

Quality policy

We are committed to consistent product quality, a superior customer experience, and continuous improvement of the products we make.
