About Us

About Us

We are a leading bus duct trunking systems manufacturer.

Our journey began in 1992 and since then, we have successfully implemented domestic and international projects. We hold an extensive track record, boasting over 5000 completed projects in over 50 countries. Cementing our position as a reliable and trusted brand in the eyes of our valued customers.

We have a strong presence in Asia Pacific and the Middle East, thanks to our network of dedicated distributors. Our strategic presence enables us to serve our customers seamlessly, no matter where they are located.

At LINKK, we strive to provide the best customer experience by exceeding expectations. Our commitment to excellence has established us as a leading provider of bus duct trunking systems.

Our Vision

We aim to be a global leader in bus duct manufacturing. Our goal is to consistently exceed expectations, providing our customers with the best bus duct systems, and excellent customer service.
