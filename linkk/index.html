Welcome to LINKK Busway System

Introduction

LINKK Busway Systems (M) Sdn Bhd, established in 2013, is a leading provider in the busway systems industry. We hold the exclusive trade mark for MEGADUCT, a trusted name that further solidifies our market position.

With 30 years of track record, Megaduct had completed 5,000 projects locally and internationally. Today, we are recognized as one of Asia’s top designer and manufacturers of Busduct Trunking Systems.

We offer a comprehensive range of busway solutions, including low, medium, and high-voltage systems, designed to cater to diverse industrial and commercial needs. Our products are engineered to adhere to the highest international standards, ensuring reliability and performance.
