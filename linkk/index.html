05 Product families

Systems we build.

LV · MV · HV

Sandwich Busduct

Engineered with precision and manufactured to meet the highest industry standards. Compact conductors laminated inside a metal housing carry far more current in far less space than equivalent cabling, and the run can be tapped anywhere along its length.

  • 99.95% min. copper
  • Short lead times
  • Flexible design
  • Tap-off anywhere
View system
NSPB · LV & MV

Non-Segregated Phase Busduct

An assembly of bus conductors with precise connections, joints and insulating supports. The conductors share one metal enclosure with no barriers between phases, which suits the heavy end of a distribution system.

  • Transformer to switchboard
  • Generator connections
  • Substation interconnection
  • Heavy industrial
