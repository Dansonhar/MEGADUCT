Research & Development

Research & development

We are dedicated to conducting market research and product innovation that meets the changing needs of our customers.

Our research and development team works tirelessly to upgrade our busway system designs, products and processes to keep pace with your needs and global demand.

What we do

Product innovation

Innovation and optimisation across the busway range, from conductor arrangement to enclosure and joint design.

Testing to 5000A

In-house temperature rise testing up to 5000A, so designs are proven under load before they reach a project.

Materials research

Development of new raw materials for bus duct manufacturing, including conductor and insulation systems.

Quality control

Every system is verified before it leaves the factory.

  • Temperature rise testing — under load, up to 5000A
  • Short circuit testing
  • Insulation and dielectric testing
  • Conductivity testing on each conductor batch
  • Earth continuity testing
  • Joint connection stability under varying loads
  • Seismic simulation for system robustness
  • Thermal imaging to locate heat points
  • Factory acceptance testing
  • Customised testing to your specification
