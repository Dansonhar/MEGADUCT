03 From material to release

Five gates, in order.

Material

Conductivity testing on each conductor batch, before anything is formed.

Manufacture

The system is built at our own factory in Beranang, Selangor.

Test

Temperature rise, short circuit, dielectric, earth continuity and joint stability.

Verify

Factory acceptance testing, plus any customised test to your own specification.

Release

Packed in installation sequence and dispatched to site.

The programme runs in our own facility, which is what makes a customised test to a client’s specification possible at all — and what lets us hold a run back rather than ship it and hope.

04 Conductor & materials

Laminated conductor stack Joint Set

What the current actually runs through.

The conductor is the product. Compact bars laminated inside a metal housing carry far more current in far less space than equivalent cabling — which only holds if the metal and the insulation between the bars are right, and that is what the materials programme exists to keep improving.

  • High-purity copper — 99.95% minimum
  • Conductivity verified per batch, not per order
  • Conductor and insulation systems are the active subject of our raw materials research
  • Joint stability tested under varying loads, because the joint is where a run fails first
