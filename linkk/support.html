01 How we work with you

Eight stages,
one supplier.

The same team stays with the project from the first review to the final audit. Nothing is handed to a third party in the middle.

Start at stage one
01

Understand the requirement

We start from your single-line diagram, load schedule and building layout, and agree the route the busduct has to take.

Agreed route and load basis
02

Design

Conductor sizing and enclosure design are set against the loads, so the run is configured correctly before anything is made.

System configuration
03

Drawings

LINKK’s proprietary design software lets our team produce professional single-line and 3D drawings in record time, showing every length, elbow, tap-off and hanger in place for your review.

Single-line and 3D drawings for sign-off
04

Manufacture

The system is built at our factory in Beranang, Selangor, including any special items your building geometry requires.

The run, made to your layout
05

Test

Every system goes through our quality control programme before dispatch — temperature rise, short circuit, dielectric, earth continuity and joint stability.

Factory acceptance testing
06

Delivery

Our meticulous bus duct packing method ensures safe and secure delivery, correctly sequenced so the run is unpacked in the order it is installed.

Packed in installation sequence
07

Installation

LINKK’s project and technical engineers supervise installation on site, so the run goes up the way the drawings said it would.

Installation supervision
08

Audit & ongoing support

Comprehensive audits of the completed work, and our people stay reachable for the life of the installation.
