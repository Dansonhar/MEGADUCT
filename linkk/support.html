Our Support

Our support

We deliver complete power distribution solutions, so you can meet your project objectives with ease — from the first drawing to the installed run.

Technical support

LINKK's proprietary design software lets our team produce professional single-line and 3D drawings in record time, so your layout can be reviewed and signed off early.

Delivery support

Our meticulous bus duct packing method ensures the safe and secure delivery of your products, correctly sequenced for installation.

Site support

LINKK's project and technical engineers offer exceptional on-site support for installation supervision and comprehensive audits.

How we work with you

  1. Understand the project

    We start from your single-line diagram, load schedule and building layout, and agree the route the busduct has to take.

  2. Design and drawings

    Our design software produces single-line and 3D drawings for your review, showing every length, elbow, tap-off and hanger in place.

  3. Manufacture and test

    The system is built at our Beranang factory and put through our quality control programme before dispatch.

  4. Delivery and installation

    Runs are packed in installation sequence and delivered to site, where our engineers supervise installation and audit the completed work.
