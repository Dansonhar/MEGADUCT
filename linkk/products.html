02 Family 01 — in production

Feeder Run Sandwich Busduct

Sandwich Busduct

Compact conductors laminated together inside a metal housing. The section carries far more current in far less space than equivalent cabling, and the run can be tapped anywhere along its length — so power can be taken off wherever a floor plan later needs it, without re-pulling cable.

  • High-purity copper — 99.95% minimum
  • Feeder and plug-in runs, with tap-off points along the length
  • Low, medium and high voltage
  • Flexible design worked around your building, not a catalogue
  • Short lead times from our own factory in Beranang

Typical applications

  • Data centres
  • Factories
  • Commercial complexes
  • Office towers
  • Hospitals
  • Public transportation

03 Family 02 — in production

End Feed Cable Box NSPB

Non-Segregated Phase Busduct

An assembly of bus conductors with precise connections, joints and insulating supports. The conductors share one metal enclosure with no barriers between phases, which suits the heavy end of a distribution system — where a transformer meets switchgear, and where standby generation connects.

  • Low and medium voltage
  • Precise connections, joints and insulating supports
  • One shared enclosure, no barriers between phases

Typical applications

  • Transformer to main switchboard
  • Generator & standby supply
  • Substation interconnection
  • Heavy industrial distribution

04 How the system works

From the transformer to the machine.

Transformer

Power arrives from the transformer or the switchboard it feeds.

Flange End

The run bolts onto the equipment here. This is where busduct meets switchgear.

Busduct

The straight run itself carries the current along its length.

Joint

Where two lengths meet and are bolted together.

Elbow / Tee

Turns the run through the building — flatwise, edgewise, offset or tee.

Tap-Off

Takes power out of the run wherever it is needed.

Equipment

Distribution boards, machines and the loads they serve.

Every stage after the transformer is manufactured by us under the MEGADUCT brand, so one supplier is responsible for the whole run rather than for one part of it.

05 System components

Every fitting, from one factory.

A run is more than its straight lengths. These are manufactured under our MEGADUCT brand and supplied with the system, so nothing on site is sourced separately.

06 Where these systems go

Matched to the building.

01Data Centres Loads change as halls are fitted out. Sandwich busduct is tapped along its length, so a new rack row is a tap-off unit rather than a new cable pull.
02Factories & Industrial Heavy, concentrated loads. Non-segregated phase busduct carries transformer-to-switchboard duty; sandwich busduct distributes to the line.
03Commercial & Office Towers Long vertical risers through the core. Sandwich busduct with vertical hangers, tapped at each floor.
04Hospitals & Assembly Lines Continuity matters more than anything. Tap-off units are interlocked so they cannot be detached while live.
05Public Transportation Long runs through structures that move and settle. Joint connection stability under varying loads is part of the test programme.
06High Rise & Mixed Development Mixed tenancies on one riser. Elbows, offsets and tees turn the run around a core that is rarely straight.
