Our Products

Sandwich bus duct

LINKK's sandwich bus ducts are engineered with precision and manufactured to meet the highest industry standards.

Compact conductors laminated together inside a metal housing carry far more current in far less space than equivalent cabling, and the run can be tapped anywhere along its length.

Advantages

  • High-purity copper — 99.95% minimum
  • Short lead times from our own factory
  • Flexible design worked around your building, not a catalogue
  • Available in low, medium and high voltage

Non-segregated phase busduct

LINKK's Non-Segregated Phase Busduct (NSPB) in low and medium voltage features an assembly of bus conductors with precise connections, joints and insulating supports.

The conductors share one metal enclosure with no barriers between phases, which suits the heavy end of a distribution system — transformer to switchgear, and generation connections.

Ask about NSPB for your project

Typical applications

  • Transformer to main switchboard connections
  • Generator and standby supply connections
  • Substation interconnections
  • Heavy industrial distribution
