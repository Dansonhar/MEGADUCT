02 Our product

Compact, light,
fast to install.

LINKK’s Non-Segregated Phase Busduct (NSPB) LV and MV feature an assembly of bus conductors with precise connections, joints, and insulating supports, all confined within a durable metal enclosure without interphase barriers. The NSPB LV-MV boasts reliable insulation properties, with bus bars that are separated and insulated with shrinkable tube.

It’s compact dimensions and relatively lightweight design make installation fast, easy, and hassle-free.
