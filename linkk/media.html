LINKK Busway Systems (M) Sdn Bhd is a leading company in busway systems, established in 2013. We own the marketing and manufacturing rights of MEGADUCT that further strengthening our position in the market.

We offer a range of busway systems, including low voltage, medium voltage and high voltage solution that catering to a wide range of industrial and commercial applications. Our products are designed to meet the highest international standards.
