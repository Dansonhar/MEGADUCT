Why MEGADUCT

Built for continuous, critical power

Continuous research and development lets us deliver uniquely reliable products — achieved through sophisticated thermodynamic design applications and quality manufacturing methods.

Thermodynamic Design

Sophisticated thermal design applications keep temperature rise within limits at full load, protecting conductor life and system reliability.

Type-Tested Assurance
