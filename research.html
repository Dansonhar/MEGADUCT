Research & Development

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

We are dedicated to conducting market research and product innovation that meets the changing needs of our customers.

Our research and development team works tirelessly to upgrade our busway system designs, products and processes to keep pace with your needs and global demand.

WHAT WE DO

Product Innovation

Continuous innovation and optimisation of the busduct range, from conductor arrangement through to enclosure and joint design.

Temperature Rise Testing

In-house temperature rise testing capability up to 5000A, so designs are proven under load before they reach a project.

Materials Research

Development of new raw materials for busduct manufacturing, including conductor and insulation systems.

QUALITY CONTROL

Every system is verified before it leaves the factory. Our test programme covers:

  • Temperature rise testing — under load, up to 5000A
  • Short circuit testing
  • Insulation and dielectric testing
  • Conductivity testing on each conductor batch
  • Earth continuity testing
  • Joint connection stability under varying loads
  • Seismic simulation for system robustness
  • Thermal imaging to locate heat points within a system
  • Factory acceptance testing
  • Customised testing to a client's own specification
